Who Can Benefit from Bridge Funding?

Bridge funding is ideal for :

  • SMEs looking to maintain cash flow
  • Businesses expanding or upgrading operations
  • Firms with delayed invoices or receivables
  • Organizations executing time-sensitive projects
  • Enterprises preparing for mergers or acquisitions

How Bridge Funding Helps Your Business

  • Maintain Business Momentum : Immediate access to capital prevents project delays, operational slowdown, and cash-flow stress.
  • Strengthen Negotiation Power : With liquidity on hand, businesses negotiate better terms with vendors, investors, and lenders.
  • Reduce Business Risk : Short-term cash flow gaps can lead to downtime, penalties, delayed orders, or loss of credibility - bridge funding minimizes these financial risks.
  • Seamless Transition to Long-Term Funding : Whether loan sanctions are pending or equity funding is under process, bridge financing fills the gap smoothly.

Eligibility Requirements

To ensure fast approvals, typical criteria include:

  • Minimum 1 year of business operations
  • Proof of incoming capital (loan, investor, receivables, contract payments, etc.)
  • Recent bank statements (6–12 months)
  • Basic business documents (GST, registration, PAN, Aadhaar)
  • Revenue consistency or confirmed project pipeline
