What Is Corporate Finance & Why It Matters

Corporate finance is the backbone of business growth. From expansion planning to capital forecasting, every major financial decision impacts long-term performance. At FinAxis® we help Indian enterprises strengthen their financial health with structured, reliable, and future-focused corporate finance solutions.

Our approach ensures your business has the liquidity, strategy, and governance needed to compete and scale confidently.

  • Improve Cash Flow & Liquidity Management: Our solutions help streamline your working capital cycle and maintain operational efficiency.
  • Support Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A): Expert guidance to evaluate, structure, and execute acquisition or merger opportunities.

Eligibility & Requirements

To ensure accurate structuring, businesses generally require:

  • Consistent revenue history.
  • Valid GST & ITR data
  • Bank statements (6–12 months)
  • Business registration documents
  • Strong financial discipline

Our Corporate Finance Offerings

Corporate Debt Structuring

We design optimized debt structures tailored to your business model, cash flows, and long-term financial strategy. Our team ensures the right balance of cost, risk, and flexibility to strengthen your capital framework.

Term Loans for Expansion

Fuel your growth with structured term loans designed for capacity building, new ventures, and business scale-up. We help secure competitive rates and flexible tenures aligned with your expansion goals.

Structured Business Capital

Customized financing solutions crafted for complex business needs. From project-linked funding to structured collateral models, we ensure efficient capital access with minimized risk.

Capital Expenditure (CapEx) Funding

Empower your business with dedicated funding for machinery, infrastructure, technology, and modernization. Our CapEx solutions support long-term asset creation and operational efficiency.

Debt Consolidation & Refinancing

Reduce your interest burden and improve cash flow by consolidating multiple debts into a single, manageable facility. We help restructure existing loans for better terms and improved financial stability.

Bridge Funding for Large Projects

Fast, short-term funding designed to bridge critical cash flow gaps, keeping large-scale projects on track until long-term financing arrives.
