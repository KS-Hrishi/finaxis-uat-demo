LET’S START SIMPLE

What is insurance, really? Not the definition ,the point of it.

Here's a simple way to think about it.

Think about everything you have spent a lifetime building. Your income. Your home. Your savings. Your family's dependence on you. Now ask yourself: what happens to all of that if something goes wrong?

Insurance is the answer to that question. It is a financial safety net ,a promise that if the unexpected happens, the financial consequences will not destroy everything you have built. It does not prevent the event. It prevents the financial ruin that follows.

Here's a thought: A 35-year-old earning ₹12 lakhs a year, with a home loan, two children, and ageing parents ,has a human life value of several crores. A term plan costing ₹12,000 a year protects all of it. Most people spend more on a weekend holiday. And yet, crores of families in India remain severely under-insured. That is not a money problem. It is an awareness problem. And that is exactly where FinAxis® begins.

COMMON QUESTIONS

The questions people are afraid to ask about insurance

A common starting point is 10 to 15 times your annual income ,enough to replace your earnings for a meaningful period and cover outstanding liabilities. But the right number depends on your specific situation. We help you calculate it properly.
A term plan is pure protection ,high cover, low premium, no maturity benefit. A traditional policy blends insurance with savings or investment, often at the cost of both. Which is right for you depends on your goals ,and that is exactly the conversation we are built for.
General insurance covers physical and operational risks ,your car, your home, your business assets, travel risks, and liability. If you own a vehicle, motor insurance is legally required. If you own a home or business, the risk of not protecting those assets is simply too high.
Online aggregators show you premiums. We show you fit. A cheaper premium with hidden exclusions is not a good deal. We analyse what you need, match it to the right product, explain every clause, and stay available when you need to use it.
