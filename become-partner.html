Is this for me?

If you know people who need funding, you can partner with us

Here's the simple idea behind it.

Every day, business owners around you struggle to arrange working capital, expand operations, or get a loan approved. You hear about these needs — from clients, friends, vendors, and your own network — long before any lender does.

As a FinAxis® partner, you simply connect that need to us. Our team evaluates the requirement, structures the right facility, and arranges funding through the most suitable bank or NBFC.

You bring the relationship. We bring the lenders, the expertise, and the process. When the case is disbursed, you earn — without ever handling documentation, underwriting, or follow-ups yourself.

It's a way to turn your network and trust into a genuine, recurring income — backed by a brand businesses already rely on.

Ways to partner

Four ways to partner with FinAxis®

Whoever you are and whatever your network looks like, there's a partnership model that fits. Pick the one that matches you best.

Referral Partner

Have contacts who need funding? Simply refer them and earn on every disbursed case. The easiest way to start — no targets, no commitments.

DSA / Channel Partner

Connector / Institutional

Agencies, fintechs, and institutions with deal flow can partner at scale with structured arrangements and dedicated coordination.

What you need to start

Basic KYC (PAN, Aadhaar), bank details for payouts, and a genuine network of people or businesses who may need finance. No office, no licence fee, no minimum targets to begin.

New to lending? That's fine.

You don't need prior experience in loans or finance. We guide you on what to refer and handle the technical side — so you can start earning from your very first referral.
