What Is First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG)?

First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG) is a structured risk-sharing framework used by NBFCs, banks, and fintechs to mitigate credit risk in loan portfolios. Under this arrangement, a third party—typically a fintech partner, corporate guarantor, or sponsor—absorbs a predefined portion of losses arising from borrower defaults.

FLDG enables lenders to expand credit responsibly while maintaining portfolio quality, capital efficiency, and investor confidence.

Key Features of FLDG Structures

Defined Risk Coverage

Coverage is limited to a predetermined percentage of the portfolio, ensuring controlled exposure.

Third-Party Risk Sharing

Fintech partners or guarantors assume first-loss responsibility up to an agreed threshold.

Capital Efficiency for Lenders

Reduced loss exposure helps optimize capital deployment and ROA.

Portfolio Performance Alignment

Encourages stronger underwriting and monitoring from all stakeholders.

Transparent Triggers & Settlement Mechanisms

Clearly defined default triggers, loss recognition, and payout timelines.
