About FinAxis

FinAxis® is India’s trusted financial partner, delivering fast, transparent, and tailored solutions for MSMEs, enterprises, and individuals. Detailed Services Information

Adequate working capital is essential for keeping your business running smoothly, and FinAxis® helps you bridge temporary cash flow gaps with flexible and fast financing. Our working capital solutions are designed for MSMEs, traders, manufacturers and service providers who require timely liquidity for inventory, payroll, supplier payments or operational expenses.

We assess your business needs and customize solutions such as OD/CC limits, invoice financing, short-term capital loans and cash-flow-based lending. With minimal documentation, faster approvals and partnerships across top banks and NBFCs, FinAxis® ensures you never miss out on growth due to cash constraints. Maintain stability, increase operational efficiency and take advantage of new opportunities with FinAxis® Working Capital Finance.
FinAxis® provides strategic corporate finance solutions that help mid-sized and large enterprises optimize capital structure and fund expansion. Our expertise includes term loans, project financing, structured debt, private credit, financial restructuring and tailored funding mechanisms that support both short-term and long-term business goals.

We partner closely with promoters and CFOs to evaluate business performance, project viability and future cash flows to recommend the most efficient financing models. Whether you're planning a new project, diversifying operations, acquiring assets or consolidating debt, FinAxis® ensures smooth execution through our strong network of financial institutions. Achieve sustainable growth with reliable, well-structured corporate finance solutions.
Bridge funding helps businesses meet urgent capital needs when long-term financing is still in progress. FinAxis® provides fast, short-term bridge loans designed to support project execution, working capital requirements, asset purchase, vendor payments or temporary cash deficits.

Our bridge funding is ideal for businesses waiting for loan disbursements, investor rounds, subsidies or receivables. With flexible tenure, minimal paperwork and rapid processing, we ensure you always stay cash-ready. FinAxis® works with leading NBFCs and private credit partners to secure strong approval rates and competitive terms, helping you maintain continuity during critical transitions.
FinAxis® offers a wide range of retail loan solutions designed for individuals seeking financial support for personal, professional or lifestyle needs. Our offerings include personal loans, home loans, loan against property, car loans, and medical or education financing.

We evaluate your profile and match you with the most suitable loan options available across our partner banks and NBFCs. With quick approval, minimal documentation and personalized advisory, FinAxis® ensures a smooth borrowing experience. Whether you're buying a new home, funding education, consolidating debt or handling an emergency, we help you secure the right finance at the right time.
FinAxis® provides comprehensive wealth management solutions designed to protect, grow and optimize your financial portfolio. Our financial experts tailor strategies based on risk profile, life goals and long-term expectations. From mutual funds and retirement planning to asset allocation, tax-efficient investments and financial advisory, we help you build a stable foundation for lifelong financial security.

With detailed planning, disciplined execution and continuous monitoring, FinAxis® ensures your wealth works effectively for you. Whether you're planning for retirement, children’s education, or long-term wealth creation, our customized strategies guide you toward financial freedom.
FinAxis® partners with NBFCs and fintech companies to provide FLDG arrangements that help credit providers expand their lending operations securely. Under FLDG, FinAxis absorbs a pre-agreed portion of the credit loss, allowing lenders to scale faster while maintaining strong risk control.

Our expertise includes portfolio structuring, risk assessment, underwriting support and compliance-ready documentation. Whether you're a lending institution or a fintech startup entering the credit space, our FLDG partnership helps you strengthen capital flow, reduce operational risk and unlock new lending opportunities.
