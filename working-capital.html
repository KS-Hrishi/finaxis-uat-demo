Eligibility Criteria

To ensure fast approvals, businesses generally need to meet:

  • Minimum ₹20–25 lakh annual turnover
  • 12+ months of business operations
  • Valid GST filings & banking history
  • Clean repayment track record (if applicable)

Documents Required

Minimal documentation for maximum convenience:

  • GST Certificate
  • Last 6–12 months Bank Statements
  • ITR (if applicable)
  • Business Proof (Shop Act / MSME / Incorporation)
  • KYC Documents (PAN, Aadhaar)

Types of Working Capital Facilities Offered by FinAxis

OverDraft

A flexible short-term credit line that allows businesses to withdraw funds beyond their account balance to meet urgent working capital needs.

Cash Credit

Continuous funding support that lets you draw up to a sanctioned limit — ideal for smooth daily operations.

Dropline Overdraft

A reducing overdraft facility tailored to match your business’s cash flow, reviewed periodically for continued support.

Lease Rental Discounting (LRD)

Secure financing using your future lease rentals as collateral — a great solution for asset-rich businesses needing liquidity.

Letter of Credit (LC)

Assures suppliers of payment while ensuring trade security — commonly used in import/export transactions.

Bank Guarantee

Provides assurance to lenders or suppliers that obligations will be met, improving your credibility and contract eligibility.

Working Capital Finance FAQs

Working capital finance helps businesses manage daily operational expenses such as inventory, salaries, rent, and receivables. It ensures smooth cash flow, prevents operational disruptions, and supports consistent business growth.
SMEs, manufacturers, traders, service providers, and growing enterprises with regular business activity and cash flow requirements are eligible. Both new-age businesses and established companies can access working capital solutions.
Working capital options include cash credit, overdraft, invoice discounting, trade finance, and short-term business loans. The right structure depends on business turnover, cash cycle, and funding needs.
Funding limits are determined based on turnover, financial statements, bank statements, and working capital cycle. Facilities can range from small limits to large structured funding for growing businesses.
Approval timelines vary by lender, but with proper documentation, working capital funding can be arranged within a few working days. Faster approvals are possible for businesses with strong financial profiles.
Collateral requirements depend on the loan structure. Some working capital facilities are unsecured, while others may require property, receivables, or personal guarantees.
Interest rates depend on business risk profile, credit history, funding structure, and lender policies. Rates may vary for secured and unsecured facilities.
Repayment can be structured as interest-only servicing, flexible drawdowns, or periodic repayments based on the chosen facility and cash flow cycle.
FinAxis® evaluates your business requirements, structures the most suitable funding option, and connects you with the right lenders to ensure fast approvals and competitive terms.
