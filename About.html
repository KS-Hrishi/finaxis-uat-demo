About FinAxis®

India’s Trusted Financial Growth Partner Since 2021

FinAxis was built on a simple belief: every individual and business in India deserves financial guidance that is honest, fast, and genuinely in their interest.

Book a Free Consultation

Founded in 2021, FinAxis® has grown into one of India’s most dependable financial advisory firms — combining deep institutional partnerships, technology-enabled processes, and an advisory-first culture to deliver real results for our clients.

We work with MSMEs, corporates, HNIs, and individuals across India — helping them access capital, grow wealth, protect assets, and build financial systems that genuinely work for the long run.

“Our focus has always been simple — do right by the client. Every approval we have secured, every portfolio we have built, every claim we have helped settle starts with that one commitment.”

— Anurag Tripathi, Founder & CEO, FinAxis®
Our Mission

Making finance simple, transparent, and empowering.

At FinAxis®, we exist to make finance and funding simple, transparent, and empowering — for individuals and businesses alike. We combine deep industry expertise, trusted partnerships, and data-driven intelligence to help our clients secure the funds to grow and build the wealth that lasts.

Finance can be complex, but it does not have to feel that way. We cut through the noise and put confident financial decision-making within reach of everyone — so you can focus on your goals while we handle the process.

Our Vision

Redefining what a financial partnership looks like.

We aspire to be one of India’s most trusted financial partners — bridging the gap between where you are today and the ambitions you are reaching toward, while making your life easier along the way.

We believe financial success is not just about numbers; it is about informed decisions, meaningful relationships, and the right services working in your favour. Looking ahead, we aim to be a lifelong advisor, helping shape a financially secure future for generations to come.
